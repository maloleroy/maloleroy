# Hello there 👋 I'm Malo

## 🤖 About me

I'm a French MEng student at [CentraleSupélec 🇫🇷](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/en) ([Paris-Saclay University](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en), 13th in the [2026 Shanghai Ranking (ARWU)](https://www.shanghairanking.com/rankings/arwu/2026) of world universities). I'm also pursuing an [MSc in Quantum, Mathematics, and Computer Science (QMI)](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/graduate-programs/masters-science/computer-science-program/masters-quantum-mathematics-computer-science) at Institut Polytechnique de Paris as a dual degree.

I'm passionate about **Quantum Computing**, and from 2025 to 2026, I worked as a research scientist intern at [IBM Quantum](https://www.ibm.com/quantum) in Germany, where I developed and benchmarked a new quantum error mitigation technique on real quantum hardware. I also explored quantum circuit synthesis for dynamical quantum algorithms.

From November 2023 to 2025, I was also part of a highly selective part-time research track at [INRIA (QuaCS team)](https://www.inria.fr/fr/quacs), France's leading research institute for digital science & technology, where I developed a new data structure for fast classical simulation of quantum algorithms. [And it's all on GitHub!](https://github.com/maloleroy/coto)

> Feel free to [check out my resume](https://github.com/maloleroy/Resume/raw/refs/heads/main/CV_Malo_Leroy.pdf) on GitHub!

From February to July 2025, I worked on exciting projects as a software engineer at [Paris Digital Lab](https://paris-digital-lab.com/), an elite gap year program where I prototyped multiple companies' most innovative projects! 🚀 (BPCE Group, SNCF, Orange)

From 2023 to 2025, I worked as a Network & DevSecOps Administrator, General Secretary, and Head of Training at [🛜 ViaRézo](https://viarezo.fr), France's largest student ISP, operating 400+ access points and 50+ switches and delivering high-speed internet access to 2,500+ students.

## 📦 Projects

- ⚛️ [Coto](https://github.com/maloleroy/coto), a cutting-edge classical simulator of quantum algorithms, using *abstract additive quantum decision diagrams*
- 🧠 [Quantum Semantics](https://github.com/maloleroy/quantum-semantics), experiments with trainable quantum circuits for GPT-like text embedding and generation
- 📊 [Open Shots Repository](https://github.com/maloleroy/openshotsrepository), a shared database of quantum circuit shots collected on real quantum hardware
- 🦀 [FHE-BPCE](https://github.com/maloleroy/fhe-bpce), a project commissioned by [BPCE Group](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/BPCE_Group) and developed by my team at Paris Digital Lab to integrate fully homomorphic encryption into cloud computing
- 🧪 [Confer](https://github.com/maloleroy/confer), a fast and memory-safe C/C++ unit testing library
- 🎶 [Eanic](https://github.com/maloleroy/eanic), a cross-platform, pure-Dart library for reading ID3 metadata from MP3 files
- *And many more! Check out the [full list](https://github.com/maloleroy?tab=repositories)*
